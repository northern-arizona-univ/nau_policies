# Residency for Tuition Purposes

In Arizona, as in all other states, tuition at the State’s publicly-supported universities is lower for in-state resident students than for out-of-state non-resident students. This reflects the fact that state universities are supported financially by the state’s taxpayers. In accordance with Arizona law, the Arizona Board of Regents establishes residency classification policy. At the time of their admission, Northern Arizona University classifies all students as either a resident or non-resident. Students must be admitted and properly registered for classes using the tuition classification initially determined by the University prior to petitioning for residency reclassification for that term.
