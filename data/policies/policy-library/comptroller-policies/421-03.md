### Purpose

To code disbursements for professional fees, independent contractor services, royalties, copyright payments, and other payments requiring tax reporting.

### Source

Internal Revenue Service
University policy

### Applicability

All payments for services, rentals, royalty, and copyright payments made to payees other than corporations, governmental agencies, colleges, and universities, excluding employee expense reimbursements and payments to nonresident aliens. Exceptions to the corporation exemption are:
medical corporations and attorneys.

### Policy

CMP 421-03: Tax Reporting IRS Form 1099

NAU provides Form 1099 to all applicable vendors for services as listed in the table below . Since tax reporting is required for professional fees, etc. for individuals or organizations other than corporations, government agencies, and colleges/universities, it is important that departments obtain accura te social security/taxpayer ID numbers, names and addresses for applicable payments/orders to new vendors.
