Policy
Under Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S.) § 41-2501.F, the Arizona Board of Regents is mandated to establish rules that outline procurement policies and procedures, ensuring they are substantially similar to those detailed in A.R.S. Chapter 23. The Arizona Board of Regents Policy Manual, specifically Article H-University Procurement Code, sets forth procurement policies for the Arizona Board of Regents in alignment with A.R.S.
requirements.
Section 3-809 of the University Procurement Code sets forth polices for legal remedies related to University Procurements. This University Policy supplements such Section 3- 809.
Section I: Responsibility
A. Contract claims shall be reported to CPRM.
B. Any Contract claim or controversy associated with a University Contract will be managed by CPRM.
