# 100329 Eligibility for Graduate Study, Faculty/Administrators

## Policy

Northern Arizona University permits administrators and faculty to obtain advanced degrees at NAU only with the approval of the employee’s supervisor and with the filing of a management plan for potential conflicts of interest**.**
