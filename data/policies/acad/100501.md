# 100501 Academic Recognition, Graduate

## Policy

### Recognition by the University

At the time of graduation from a master's or doctoral degree program, if the student has earned a cumulative grade point average of 3.90 or higher for all courses taken at Northern Arizona University, NAU will recognize the student with the notation "*with distinction*" on the student's transcript.
