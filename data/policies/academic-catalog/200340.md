# 200340 Exploratory (Undeclared) Policy Regarding Declaration of Major Within a Specific Timeframe

## Policy

Undergraduate students are required to declare a major by the end of their third term at NAU. Students are required to take a support course in each of their first two terms as long as they remain an Exploratory student.
