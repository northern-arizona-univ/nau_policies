# 100317 Applying for Re-Admission, Undergraduate

## Policy

If you are a former, degree-seeking NAU student who has not been enrolled in any of the last three consecutive terms, you must apply for re-admission. In determining three consecutive terms, we count Fall-Spring-Fall or Spring-Fall-Spring sequences during which you enrolled. You can also use the Summer or Winter terms to maintain your enrollment status (though if you don't enroll during the Summer or Winter terms, that isn't counted against you). (Former Non-degree students may be readmitted as Non-degree only.)

Please note that to be considered enrolled for any term, you must have received a grade for at least one course, and paid all tuition and fees for that term or have had arrangements for such payments approved by the Office of Student Financial Aid.

If you have taken courses at another college or university since you last enrolled at NAU, you are required to submit official transcripts from those institutions to receive transfer credit.

If you do not meet the above criteria for re-admission, please go to the [Undergraduate Admissions](https://home.nau.edu/admissions/default.asp) website and complete an Undergraduate Admissions application.

### Personalized Learning

If you are a former, degree-seeking NAU Personalized Learning student who has not been in an active subscription within the last 365 calendar days, you must apply for re-admission.

Please note that to be considered enrolled for any subscription period, you must have paid all tuition for that subscription period or have had arrangements for such payments approved by the Office of Student Financial Aid.

If you have taken courses at another college or university since you last enrolled at NAU, you are required to submit official transcripts from those institutions to receive transfer credit.

If you do not meet the above criteria for re-admission, please go to the Undergraduate Admissions website and complete an Undergraduate Admissions application.

## General Procedure

Please see the [Application for Re-admission (Undergraduate)](http://nau.edu/Registrar/Forms-and-Policies/#Enrollment) in the Enrollment & Petition Forms section.

## Special Circumstances

If you have been academically suspended, you must fill out an Appeal for Reinstatement and submit the completed form to the [Gateway Student Success Center](http://www4.nau.edu/gateway/).

Information on reinstatement and the Reinstatement Appeal Form can be found on the Gateway Student Success Center website. Academic suspension may occur at the end of the Fall, Spring, or Summer terms. You will not be permitted to return to the university for at least one full term (Fall, Spring, or Summer) following an academic suspension.

Reinstatement Appeals are due in writing by the deadline for the term you wish to return.   Documents must be received, not postmarked, in the [Gateway Student Success Center](http://www4.nau.edu/gateway/) by the end of the business day for the deadline listed. Late appeals will not be considered. Please visit the [Gateway Student Success Center](http://www4.nau.edu/gateway/) for additional information on the appeal process. If you need assistance by phone, please call the Gateway Student Success Center at 928-523-4772**.**

If you are reinstated through Academic Renewal and the process results in a GPA of 2.0 or above, the requirement to return on academic probation will be waived.

### Honorably Discharged Military

Effective for the Fall 2011 term and terms following, students who have been honorably discharged from the U.S. Armed Forces and meet the conditions for in-state tuition as outlined in statute A.R.S. § 15-1802 will need to:

- provide a copy of a DD-214 stating “Honorable Discharge” and either of the following:
- proof of voter registration in the state of Arizona; or
- a copy of an Arizona driver’s license

to the NAU Office of Military and Veteran Affairs by the start date of their re-admission term.
