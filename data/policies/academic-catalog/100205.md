# 100205 Academic Plans, Graduate Degrees and Subplans (Definitions)

## Policy

An academic plan is a particular course of study identified by Northern Arizona University, in accordance with standards recommended by the Arizona Board of Regents ([ABOR Policy](https://www.azregents.edu/about/policy-manual) 2-221)

### Doctoral and Master's Degrees

### Academic Program Plan

- Doctoral degree programs at NAU require a minimum of 60 units. Many doctoral degree programs require more than 60 units, depending on discipline and/or external accreditation standards.
  - The Medical Science DMSc program is an exception, requiring only 24 units, due to completion of a Physician Assistant Studies Master's degree, which requires over 100 units of credit.

- Master’s degree programs at NAU require a minimum of 30 units. Many master’s degree programs require more than 30 units, depending on discipline and/or external accreditation standards.

At least 12 units within the academic program plan should be unique to that academic program plan to differentiate it from other academic program plans**.**

### Emphasis

If needed, emphases can be judiciously created for an academic program plan.  An emphasis is a planned group of courses, consisting of at least 9 units, from one or more subject matter areas, related to the academic program plan’s discipline. Emphases within an academic program plan must differ from each other by a minimum of 9 units. Furthermore, emphases may not require more than 60% of the total number of credits for the academic program plan.

An example of an emphasis (sub-plan) printed on transcripts is as follows:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Degree | Master of Science |
| Confer Date | 2017-08-02 |
| Plan | Chemistry (MS) |
| Sub-Plan | Carcinogenesis and Cancer Chemotherapy - Emphasis |

### Concentration

An academic program plan may also label smaller groupings of courses as concentrations. Concentrations will not be printed on the transcript. A concentration represents a lesser subdivision within an academic program plan than an emphasis, but serves the same purpose of allowing students to tailor the academic program plan to their needs and interests.
