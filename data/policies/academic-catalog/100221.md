# 100221 Undergraduate Student Classifications

## Policy

### Undergraduate Student Classifications

If you have earned up to 29 units by the close of the preceding term, the Registrar's Office classifies you as a freshman; if you have 30 to 59 units, you are considered a sophomore; 60 to 89 units, a junior; and 90 or more units, a senior.

| Number of Units | Academic Level |
| --- | --- |
| 0-29 | Freshman |
| 30-59 | Sophomore |
| 60-89 | Junior |
| 90 or more | Senior |
