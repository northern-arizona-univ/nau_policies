# 100208 Merged Major, Undergraduate (Definitions)

## Policy

One major may require courses that are also required in another major. You can then earn these two majors as a merged major, which means you can complete the requirements for both majors with fewer units than if the majors were completed separately. Specific merged majors are described under individual disciplines in the [Northern Arizona University Academic Catalog](http://catalog.nau.edu/Catalog/).
