### Policy

TRV 565-03: Maximum Reimbursement Rates

1. Reimbursement rates for in-state long-term travel are established and listed in the State of Arizona Travel Policy Supplement I.

2. Reimbursement rates for out-of-state long-term travel will be determined on a case by case basis.
Please contact the Comptroller’s Office for guidance.
