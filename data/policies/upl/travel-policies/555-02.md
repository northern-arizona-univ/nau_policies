### Policy

TRV 555-02: Travel within 100 Miles of the Arizona Border

1. Travel to U.S. states within 100 miles of the border with Arizona may be deemed in -state travel at the discretion of department leadership.

2. The decision to treat a travel transaction as in-state instead of out of state may impact:
a. Account coding
b. The need to prepare a TA
c. Use of a personal vehicle
d. Maximum reimbursement rates for M&IE and lodging

3. The in-state travel rules must be applied consistently across the travel transaction.

4. Single day and extended day reimbursements should be treated as in-state travel.
