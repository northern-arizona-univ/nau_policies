### Policy

TRV 545-02: Trip Approval

1. An employee must obtain prior approval for out-of-state travel from department leadership. This approval may be electronic or written.

2. Travel to an out-of-state destination must be transacted in accordance with TRV 525-01: Ground Transportation and TRV 530-01: Air Transportation policies.
