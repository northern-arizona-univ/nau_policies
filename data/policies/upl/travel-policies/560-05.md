### Purpose

To provide policy on the treatment of travel reimbursements that have the potential of the appearance of personal benefit.

### Policy

TRV 560-05: Optional Event Registration Fees

1. Optional registration fees such as those for recreational or social activities should not be reimbursed unless they can be tied to a specific business purpose and supported with documentation.
