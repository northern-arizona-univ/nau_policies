### Introduction

NAU's fiscal year ends on June 30th. Financial Accounting Servi ces (FAS) is responsible for establishing and coordinating fiscal year-end processing deadlines and proce dures to ensure that financial transactions are posted timely and accurately for financial reporting purpos es. Departments are responsible for adhering to the year-end deadlines and procedures.
