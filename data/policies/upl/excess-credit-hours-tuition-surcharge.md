### Excess Credit Hours Tuition Surcharge

### Policy Summary

As mandated by Arizona law, undergraduate students who have accumulated 145 or more units of academic credit (“credit hours”) are, with certain exceptions, assessed excess credit hours tuition surcharges in addition to regular tuition, fees, and other applicable charges. Information regarding the applicable surcharges is available on the Student and Departmental Account Services website. Students are encouraged to speak with their academic advisor if they have questions about the excess credit hours tuition surcharge. Students who believe they have been misidentified as exceeding the Credit Hour Threshold or that they may qualify for one of the allowable exceptions may petition for re-evaluation.

### Reason for This Policy

Thi s policy is necessary to implement certain mandates that require students who attend a public university in Arizona to pay tuition surcharges after they accumulate a certain number of credit hours.

### Definitions

Credit Hour Threshold: the number of units of academic credit, expressed as “credit hours,” an undergraduate student may earn before having to pay excess credit hours tuition and fee surcharges. The current Credit Hour Threshold is 145 credit hours. Certain exceptions apply as outlined below.

### Policy

A. Credit Hour Threshold
Undergraduate students who enroll for credit hours beyond the Credit Hour Threshold are required by the Arizona Board of Regents to pay an excess credit hours tuition surcharge. This surcharge is assessed in addition to regular tuition, fees, and any other applicable payments.

B. Exceptions
T he undergraduate Credit Hour Threshold shall not apply to degree programs that require credit hours above the Credit Hour Threshold or to credits:
1. Ear ned in pursuit of up to two (2) baccalaureate degrees;
2. E arned in pursuit of up to two (2) state-regulated licensures and certificates;
3. E arned in pursuit of teaching certification;
4. T ransferred from a private institution of higher education;
5. T ransferred from an institution of higher education in another state;
6. E arned at another institution of higher education but not accepted as transfer credits; and
7. E arned by students who enroll more than twenty-four (24) months after the end of that student’s previous enrollment at a public institution of higher education in Arizona.
C. St ate-Regulated Licensures and Certificates
T he state-regulated licensures and certificates that qualify for the exemption enumerated in Section 2(b) above include, but are not limited to:
- C omputer Science
- Dental Hygiene
- Engineering (not including Construction Management)
- Nursing
- Accountancy or Public Accounting
- Social Work
D. T eaching Certifications
T he teaching certifications that qualify for the exemption enumerated in Section 2(c) above include, but are not limited to:
- A rt Education
- Career and Technical Education
- Elementary Education
- Health or Physical Education
- Music Education
- Physical Education

F. Request for Re-Evaluation
Students who: i) have been notified by the Office of the Registrar as exceeding the Credit Hour Threshold, or ii) believe they may qualify for one of the exceptions outlined in Section 2 above, may submit an Excess Credit Hours Re-Evaluation Request Form to request a review of their credit hours and related matters. Upon receiving such requests, the Office of the Registrar, assisted as necessary and appropriate by other units, will perform the re-evaluation and will notify the student of the results in a timely manner.

### Responsibilities

Academic Advisors: work to ensure that students are aware of this policy and the potential for the assessment of excess credit hour surcharges; assist students in reviewing coursework requirements to prevent or minimize their need to breach the threshold.
Institutional Research and Analysis: each semester, identify students approaching and exceeding the Credit Hour Threshold and inform the Office of the Registrar accordingly.
Office of the Registrar: notify students approaching the Credit Hour Threshold of this policy and the potential for excess credit hour surcharges; appropriately track and notify students who have exceeded the Credit Hour Threshold; upon request, perform excess credit hour reviews and provide timely notification of the results.
Student and Departmental Account Services: provide notification via its website of applicable excess credit hour surcharges; properly assess applicable excess credit hour surcharges.
