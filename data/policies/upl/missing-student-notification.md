Missing Student Notification

Definitions
Flagstaff Resident Student: A student living in student housing facilities on the Flagstaff mountain campus.
NAU-Yuma Resident Student: A NAU student living in student housing facilities on the Arizona Western College campus.
Policy
A. It is the policy of Northern Arizona University to investigate any report of a missing resident student (student who resides in on-campus student housing facilities).
B. All resident students are requested to identify a contact person or persons whom the institution will notify within 24 hours of a law enforcement agency determining that the student is missing. This contact information will be registered confidentially and is accessible only to authorized campus officials who will not disclose it except to law enforcement personnel in furtherance of a missing person investigation.
C. Reporting a Missing Student
Anyone that receives a report of a missing student must immediately bring it to NAU Police Department (NAUPD) or the appropriate local law enforcement agency’s attention.
1. Flagstaff Resident Students
If a member of the university community or any other person has reason to believe that a Flagstaff Resident Student has been missing from campus for 24 hours, or less if suspicious circumstances exist, they should immediately notify NAUPD. NAUPD will initiate a missing person report and investigate in collaboration with the Office of the Dean of Students and Campus Living and/or affiliates such as American Campus Communities (ACC).

2. NAU-Yuma Resident Students
If a member of the university community or any other person has reason to believe that a NAU-Yuma Resident Student has been missing from campus for 24 hours, or less if suspicious circumstances exist, they should immediately notify Arizona Western College Police Department (AWCPD).
D. Missing Student Notification
If NAUPD or AWCPD determine that a student can be designated a missing person, the Dean of Students or their designee will notify the student’s designated contact person no later than 24 hours after the student is determined to be missing. If the student is under 18 years of age and not emancipated, the Dean of Students or their designee will also notify the student’s custodial parent or guardian within 24 hours that the student is missing.
NAUPD will notify the local law enforcement agency (that has jurisdiction in the area that the student is missing) within 24 hours of the determination that the student is missing, unless the local law enforcement agency was the entity that determined that the student is missing.
