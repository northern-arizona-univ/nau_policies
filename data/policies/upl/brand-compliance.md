# Brand Compliance

A strong, cohesive brand is essential to NAU’s reputation and its ability to deliver a consistent, meaningful experience. This policy establishes standards for using NAU’s visual and verbal brand components; procedures for representing the brand through naming, marks, and slogans; and requirements for on-campus brand presence. It ensures that all stakeholders—employees, students, and partners—consistently uphold the brand across all channels and audiences.
