Eligibility
Under the Parental Leave of Absence Policy regular benefit eligible employees are eligible to request up to twelve weeks approved time off away from work for the purpose of birth and bonding. Benefits are also available in the case of miscarriage or still birth.
- Paid time off: Regular benefit eligible employees with at least one year of service and who has worked at least 1250 hours in the previous twelve months can request up to 12 weeks of approved paid time off.
- Unpaid time off: Regular employees with less than one year of service and/or who has worked less than 1250 hours in the previous 12 months can request up to 12 weeks of approved unpaid time off. For you to receive pay for your time away from work you are required to report accrued time off on your timesheet in the following order: sick, vacation, compensatory time.
Parental leave runs concurrently with Family Medical Leave.
